# aktujo-core

The domain of aktujo, without I/O: what a title, a label, the name of a space or of a device may be, identities of
kinds made from their names, and the events of a space that are not about documents (its name, its kinds' place in
the log). `aktujo-doc` holds the documents.

Part of [aktujo](https://github.com/aktujo/aktujo) – used by [`aktujo-sdk`](https://docs.rs/aktujo-sdk). The design
is in [RFC 0001](https://github.com/aktujo/aktujo/blob/main/docs/rfc/0001-architecture.md) and
[RFC 0006](https://github.com/aktujo/aktujo/blob/main/docs/rfc/0006-apps-and-sdk.md).
